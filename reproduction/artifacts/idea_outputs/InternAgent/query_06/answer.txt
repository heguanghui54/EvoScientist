The research proposal introduces the Typology-Aware Self-Supervised Adaptation with Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (TASSA-PEFT) framework. It addresses the triadic constraints of data scarcity, language diversity, and acoustic variability through three components: (1) a multi-source, typology-aware self-supervised pretraining phase that selects and combines encoders from language families similar to the target language using a learned weighted fusion layer; (2) a hybrid Dynamic Adapter-LoRA (DAL) module for parameter-efficient fine-tuning with dynamic placement based on layer-wise similarity analysis; and (3) a contrastive prototypical adjustment mechanism to detect and compensate for negative transfer without requiring linguistic annotations.
