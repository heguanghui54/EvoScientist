**1. Title**
Hybrid Linear-Recurrent Attention with Dynamic Gating for 1M+ Token Contexts.

**2. Problem and Motivation**
Standard Transformer architectures utilize Softmax attention, which scales quadratically ($O(n^2)$) in both time and memory relative to sequence length. While techniques like Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) interpolation and Sliding Window Attention (SWA) extend context limits, they often suffer from the "lost-in-the-middle" phenomenon or significant performance degradation in retrieval-intensive tasks. State Space Models (SSMs) like Mamba offer linear scaling ($O(n)$) but struggle with high-precision, many-to-many dependency modeling compared to traditional attention.

**3. Core Hypothesis**
Integrating a selective SSM (Mamba-based) as a global context compressor alongside a sparse, local-attention mechanism—linked by a learnable dynamic gating unit—will maintain high retrieval accuracy (Needle-in-a-Haystack) at 1M+ tokens while reducing computational overhead by at least 40% compared to full-attention models.

**4. Method**
*   **Architecture**: A hybrid block structure where every $N$-th layer (e.g., $N=4$) is a standard Multi-Head Attention (MHA) layer with RoPE, and the intermediate layers are Selective SSM (Mamba) blocks.
*   **Dynamic Gating**: Implement a "Context Gate" that computes a weighted sum of the SSM hidden state and the local attention output. The gate is conditioned on the current token's entropy, allowing the model to prioritize high-precision attention for dense information and SSM for background context.
*   **Position Encoding**: Use "YaRN" (Yet another RoPE extension method) for the attention layers to ensure smooth extrapolation to lengths beyond the training window.
*   **Training**: Two-stage curriculum learning. Stage 1: Continued pre-training on 32k sequences. Stage 2: Fine-tuning on 128k–1M sequences using LongLoRA or similar parameter-efficient methods.

**5. Dataset or Benchmark**
*   **Training**: SlimPajama-6B (subset) and specialized long-context synthetic data (e.g., book summarization).
*   **Evaluation**:
    *   **LongBench**: Comprehensive multi-task long-context benchmark.
    *   **RULER**: A retrieval-based benchmark specifically designed to test the effective context window.
    *   **Needle-In-A-Haystack**: To measure precise information retrieval at varying depths.

**6. Evaluation Metrics**
*   **Retrieval Accuracy (%)**: Success rate in the Needle-In-A-Haystack test.
*   **Perplexity (PPL)**: Measured across increasing context lengths to check for "explosion" or degradation.
*   **Latency/Throughput**: Tokens per second (TPS) and Peak VRAM usage during inference.
*   **ROUGE-L / F1**: For long-form summarization and multi-document QA tasks.

**7. Baselines**
*   **Llama-3-8B (RoPE-scaled)**: Standard Transformer with extrapolated position embeddings.
*   **Mamba-2.8B**: Pure SSM baseline.
*   **LongLoRA / FlashAttention-2**: Current state-of-the-art implementations for efficient long-context Transformers.

**8. Ablations**
*   **Hybrid Ratio**: Compare performance of 1:1, 1:3, and 1:7 ratios of Attention to SSM layers.
*   **Gating Mechanism**: Compare static weighting vs. the proposed dynamic entropy-based gating.
*   **Positional Bias**: Test performance with and without YaRN-scaled embeddings in the hybrid setup.

**9. Failure Modes and Feasibility Risks**
*   **State Drift**: SSMs can accumulate errors over very long sequences, leading to "hallucinations" in the latter half of a 1M token document.
*   **Hardware Compatibility**: Mamba kernels are highly optimized for specific GPU architectures (A100/H100); porting the hybrid model to consumer hardware may result in sub-linear speedups.
*   **Training Divergence**: Mixing recurrent and attention gradients can lead to instability during the second stage of fine-tuning.
