Research Proposal: Reproducing and Extending Masked Audio Modeling for
General-Purpose Audio Foundation Models## Title
Are Masked Autoencoders
Sufficient for General-Purpose Audio Representation Learning? A Reproducibility
and Extension Study of AudioMAE

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Problem
Self-supervised audio foundation models promise a single pretrained
backbone that transfers to speech, music, and environmental sound tasks. The
AudioMAE (Xu et al.,2022) architecture—an asymmetric encoder-decoder with a high
mask ratio (75–80%) applied to mel-spectrogram patches—claimed state-of-the-art
transfer on AudioSet, ESC-50, and Speech Commands v2. However, the community
lacks:

1 Independent reproduction — the original code was released late and the
results have not been systematically verified by a separate group.
2 A controlled comparison against reconstruction-based (AudioMAE) vs.
contrastive (CLAP, ImageBind) pretraining objectives for general-purpose use.
3 An ablation isolating what drives performance: high mask ratio, decoder
depth, or patch size?

We aim to reproduce AudioMAE's core results and extend them by introducing a
hybrid objective that combines masked reconstruction with a contrastive
alignment loss (audio-text), testing the hypothesis that reconstruction alone is
insufficient for general-purpose transfer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hypothesis
Null hypothesis (H₀): AudioMAE's masked reconstruction objective
yields representations that transfer to speech, music, and environmental tasks
as well as or better than a hybrid reconstruction + contrastive objective.

Alternative hypothesis (H₁): Adding a contrastive alignment loss (audio-text)
during pretraining significantly improves downstream transfer on tasks requiring
high-level semantics (e.g., audio captioning, zero-shot sound classification),
while maintaining or improving performance on acoustic-only tasks (e.g., scene
classification).

Falsification condition: If the hybrid model does not outperform AudioMAE on at
least2 out of3 semantic-level downstream tasks (by >2% absolute in the primary
metric) while not regressing by more than1% on acoustic-level tasks, H₁ is
rejected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Method
### Stage1 — Reproduce AudioMAEExact reproduction of the AudioMAE baseline
(Xu et al.,2022) using the official configuration:

• Input: Log-mel spectrograms (128 mel bins,1024 FFT,512 hop), patchified
into16×16 patches (time×freq).
• Encoder: ViT-B/16 (12 layers,768 hidden,12 heads).
• Decoder: Lightweight4-layer ViT (384 hidden,6 heads).
• Mask ratio:80% of patches masked.
• Pretraining objective: L2 reconstruction loss in the spectrogram space
(normalized patches).
• Pretraining data: AudioSet-2M (balanced subset of ~2M clips from AudioSet).
• Optimization: AdamW, lr=1.5e-4, cosine decay, batch size1024,800 epochs.

Stage2 — Hybrid Objective (AudioMAE + CLAP-style contrastive head)

Attach a contrastive head to the [CLS] token of the encoder. During
pretraining,50% of batches use reconstruction loss and50% use the InfoNCE loss
aligning audio embeddings with paired text descriptions (from AudioSet labels +
synthetic captions via LaCLAP pipeline). Total loss: L = λ·L_recon +
(1-λ)·L_contrastive, with λ annealed from1.0 →0.5 across training.

Stage3 — Downstream transfer evaluationFreeze encoder, train linear probes (and
separately, full fine-tuning) on each benchmark.
